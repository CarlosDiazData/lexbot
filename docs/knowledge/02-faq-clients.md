# Frequently Asked Questions — Clients

## What should I bring to the first consultation?
Bring a government-issued ID, any contracts or documents related to your case, and a summary of the timeline of events.

## How are fees calculated?
Fees depend on the type of matter: fixed fee for standard procedures, hourly rate for complex work, or contingency for certain civil cases. You receive a written fee agreement before any work begins.

## Can I get advice by phone or Telegram?
Yes. Routine questions are answered through the Telegram business line during office hours. Anything requiring legal analysis is scheduled as a consultation.

## How long does a contract review take?
Standard contract reviews are delivered within 5 business days. Urgent reviews are available at a premium rate.

## What happens during the first consultation?
The first consultation is an opportunity to explain your situation, understand your goals, and receive an initial assessment. The lawyer will outline the options available and, when appropriate, recommend a next step.

## Do you charge for the first consultation?
For most matters the first consultation is free of charge. Matters that require significant preparation or document review before the meeting may be billed at the standard hourly rate.

## How do I prepare for the first consultation?
Gather every document related to your case, write down the key dates, and list the questions you want answered. The more context you bring, the more useful the consultation will be.

## What should I do if I need urgent legal help?
Contact the office by phone and state that the matter is urgent. The intake team will connect you with the lawyer on duty. Emergency matters are assessed the same day.
