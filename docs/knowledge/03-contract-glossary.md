# Contract Glossary

## Retainer
A retainer is an advance payment that secures the firm's availability and is applied against future invoices.

## Force majeure
Force majeure covers unforeseeable events outside a party's control that prevent contractual performance.

## Indemnity
An indemnity is a contractual obligation to compensate the other party for specified losses.

## Jurisdiction
Jurisdiction determines which courts or arbitration bodies resolve disputes arising from the contract.

## Termination
Termination clauses define how a contract can be ended before its natural expiry, including notice periods and the consequences of early termination.

## Breach of contract
A breach occurs when a party fails to perform a duty imposed by the contract, whether by failing to deliver, paying late, or acting against the agreed terms.

## Liquidated damages
Liquidated damages are a pre-agreed sum payable when a specific breach occurs, set at the time the contract is signed rather than assessed after the fact.

## Governing law
The governing law clause states which legal system interprets the contract. It is often paired with the jurisdiction clause and should be chosen deliberately.

## Severability
A severability clause keeps the rest of the contract enforceable if a court strikes down one provision as invalid or unenforceable.
